\section{Hardware SPI Configuration}
\label{sec:spi-config}

The EEPROM is driven by the STM32 hardware SPI peripheral, configured entirely
through its registers (no HAL/LL SPI functions or CubeMX configuration).

\paragraph{SPI clock calculation.}
The SPI kernel clock is PCLK1 $=$ \PCLKHz~Hz. The baud-rate field BR[2:0] of
\reg{SPI\_CR1} divides it by $2^{\mathrm{BR}+1}$~\cite{rm0091}:
\begin{equation}
  f_\mathrm{SCK} = \frac{f_\mathrm{PCLK1}}{2^{\mathrm{BR}+1}}
                 = \frac{\text{\PCLKHz~Hz}}{\text{\BRDivider}}
                 = \text{\fSCKPred~kHz}.
  \label{eq:sck}
\end{equation}
